Hi, thanks for stopping by.

This blog is mostly about things I build, technologies I use, and questions that stay interesting after a project has worked for the first time.

## What you will find here

Expect posts about backend engineering, machine learning, computer vision, infrastructure, testing, and the useful lessons that usually appear only after the first implementation.

I will also use the blog to share selected project work. That includes [PacePilot](/projects), my LLM-assisted running coach, infrastructure work around Kubernetes and GitOps, and insights from my master's thesis on data drift in industrial image processing systems.

The goal is not to make every project look perfect. It is to explain the actual decisions, trade-offs, and failures behind the result.

## How I use AI

AI is part of my workflow, but it does not replace my judgment or source work.

- **Writing:** I use AI to refine sentences and catch unclear phrasing.
- **Coding:** I see AI as a pair programmer, helping me explore solutions and generate boilerplate code.
- **Thinking:** It can be useful for brainstorming or exploring a technical question from another angle.
- **Research:** Important claims are still checked against primary sources, documentation, and implementation evidence.

I will not publish AI-generated posts as if they were my own finished thoughts. Text here is written, reviewed, and maintained by me. Keeping thinking as a human responsibility is important to me, and I hope it is to you as well.
