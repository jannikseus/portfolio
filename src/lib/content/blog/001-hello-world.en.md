Hi, thanks for stopping by!

This blog is mostly about things I build, technologies I use, and questions that stay interesting even after a project finally works. Every now and then there might also be a personal topic in here, just because I find it interesting.

## What you will find here

Expect posts about backend engineering, machine learning, computer vision, infrastructure, testing, and all those lessons that only show up after the first implementation.

I will also share selected project work. The goal is not to make every project look perfect, but to explain the actual decisions, trade-offs, and failures behind the result.

## How I use AI

AI is part of my workflow, but it does not replace my judgment or my source work. I am still responsible for what I publish and for whether it is correct. I use AI in four main ways:

- **Writing:** AI helps me refine sentences and catch unclear phrasing. As a non-native English speaker, this is super useful. It polishes my wording, but the ideas and structure are mine.
- **Coding:** Think of it as a pair programmer that helps me explore solutions and churn out boilerplate.
- **Thinking:** Me human. Me thinking. Brainstorming with AI can be useful, but I still want to use my own brain. Lol.
- **Research:** Important claims still get checked against primary sources, documentation, and actual implementation evidence.

As you can probably guess, I am not a fan of hyping AI to the fullest or outsourcing every single thought to a language model. For me, AI is a tool, not a replacement for thinking. I started programming because I enjoy thinking about problems and building solutions, and I want to keep it that way for as long as I can. (Probably a hot take for some people, but that's how it is.)

Still, I do have to engage with the fast-moving world of AI, from assisted software engineering to agentic development and software factories. Will I slowly become a software architect? I will write about that too, but I will keep it grounded in my own experience and observations.

I will not publish AI-generated posts and pass them off as my own finished thoughts. Everything here is written, reviewed, and maintained by me. Thinking is a human responsibility, and I hope you see it the same way.

Until then, happy coding, and I hope you enjoy reading my posts as much as I enjoy writing them!
